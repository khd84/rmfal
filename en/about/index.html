Almaymoni & Alalwani Law Firm (RMFAL)

At Almaymoni & Alalwani Law Firm (RMFAL), we always have and continue to deliver our clients innovative, effective, and individually tailored legal services. We work side by side with our clients to help them achieve their goals, reclaim their rights, and grow their businesses. Our professional, highly skilled, and knowledgeable team is committed to providing exemplary legal representation and consultation to our clients in various legal aspects. We excel at providing integrated corporate and commercial law, contracts, banking and finance, real estate, property management, criminal defense, family law, labor law, and other legal services. We also provide litigation and pleading services before courts and bodies of all types and represent clients before all judicial, administrative, and governmental bodies. Additionally, we can provide most of our services virtually.

Mission

Achieving the mission of the lawyer by consolidating the principles of justice to achieve professional foundations in our dealings with success partners.

Vision

Leadership in the field of presenting legal work from a new perspective and exceptionally and in line with the vision of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia 2030, which aspires to enrich legal awareness.

Our Goals

Providing comprehensive and flexible legal services while adhering to the highest professional standards and efficiency to be one of the most competitive legal entities in the Saudi market.
